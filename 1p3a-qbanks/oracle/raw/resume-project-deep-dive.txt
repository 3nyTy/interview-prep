Resume / Project Deep Dive (HM or Tech Screen Opener)
Prepare two or three projects for a resume-led deep dive covering technical challenges, architecture choices, ownership, customer communication, on-call work, and questions about the team's business architecture.

Stage
phone-screen · onsite-deep-dive
Requirements

Prepare two or three projects that can support a detailed technical conversation.

Be ready to explain the core technical challenge, the architecture choices made, and the trade-offs behind those choices.

Show personal ownership through concrete decisions and outcomes rather than describing only the team's work.

Include examples involving direct customer communication and on-call or incident-response work.

Prepare follow-up questions about the team's business architecture.

Notes

Use a consistent story shape: context and constraints, architecture, your decisions, alternatives rejected, measurable outcome, and what you would change now.

Keep the system boundary and your personal contribution explicit so the interviewer can move between technical depth and ownership without losing the thread.

Preparation

Build a one-page matrix mapping each selected project to its technical challenge, architecture choice, ownership decision, customer impact, and operational lesson; fill every cell with one concrete fact.

Rehearse a two-minute architecture walkthrough and a five-minute deep dive for each project, including one rejected alternative and one follow-up improvement.

Write three questions about the target team's business architecture and practice connecting each question to a relevant decision from one of your projects.
