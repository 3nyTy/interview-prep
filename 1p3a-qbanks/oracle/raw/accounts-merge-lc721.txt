Accounts Merge (LC 721)
Implement the original LC 721 Accounts Merge prompt: merge account records that share an email address and return each person's name with the sorted set of emails in the connected group.

Requirements

Each account record contains a name followed by one or more email addresses.

Two account records belong to the same person when they share at least one email address, including transitively through other records.

Merge every connected group into one record containing the person's name and all distinct email addresses in sorted order.

The output records may be returned in any order.

Notes

The onsite used the original LeetCode 721 prompt. Equal names alone do not establish identity; connectivity is determined by shared email addresses.

Model emails as nodes. Either union all emails in each account with a disjoint-set structure or connect them in an adjacency list and traverse components. Group emails by component, sort each group, then prepend the associated name.

With E email occurrences, disjoint-set operations are near-linear and sorting dominates at O(E log E) time; maps, component storage, and the output use O(E) auxiliary space.

Clean implementation is the central difficulty. In one successful IC4 loop, a mostly complete implementation was accepted after the remaining details were explained verbally.

Preparation

Implement both union-find and graph-traversal versions, then test transitive overlap, duplicate names with no shared email, isolated accounts, and repeated email occurrences.

Rehearse a five-minute walkthrough covering the component invariant, where the name is stored, why emails are deduplicated, and why sorting determines the final time bound.
