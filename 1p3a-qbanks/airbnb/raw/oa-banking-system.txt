Progressive Banking System OA
Build a four-level banking system. The progression covers account creation, deposits and transfers; ranking the highest-spending accounts; scheduling and canceling future payments; and finally merging accounts while retaining balances, transaction state, and historical queries. A workspace-credit rotation replaces scheduled payments with delayed cashback while preserving the same state-ordering challenge.

Requirements

Implement a banking system whose levels unlock progressively.

Level 1:

create_account(timestamp, account_id).

deposit(timestamp, account_id, amount).

transfer(timestamp, source_account_id, target_account_id, amount).

Handle missing accounts, duplicate creation, identical source and target accounts, insufficient funds, and the required return balance.

Level 2:

top_spenders(timestamp, n) ranks accounts by total outgoing value.

A transfer increases only the source account's outgoing total.

Apply the supplied deterministic tie-break rule when outgoing totals match.

Level 3:

Schedule a future payment.

Cancel a scheduled payment.

Query or otherwise track payment status.

Process payments that are due before any later operation observes or mutates the current state.

Successful scheduled payments also contribute to the Level 2 outgoing total.

Level 4:

Merge accounts while retaining both balances, transaction histories, spending histories, and pending-payment information.

Query an account's historical balance at a specified time.

Notes

Time-dependent work must be settled before executing operations whose results depend on the current state. A single settlement routine invoked by earlier operations keeps this behavior consistent.

Later levels depend directly on the state and accounting decisions made in earlier levels.

Account merge semantics require a deliberate policy for references to the retired account ID; follow the supplied interface and tests.

Exact return types, the ranking tie-break, payment-ID format, and ordering among payments sharing a timestamp come from the assessment contract.

Alternate canonical variant — Workspace credits and delayed cashback

Level 1 creates workspaces, adds credits, and transfers credits between workspaces.

Level 2 ranks workspaces by cumulative outgoing or consumed credits.

Level 3 spends credits and schedules a 2% cashback to arrive 24 hours later; the cashback status is queryable by transaction ID.

Level 4 merges workspaces, carries over balances and transaction history, redirects pending cashback, and supports point-in-time balance queries.

Preparation

Implement account creation, deposits, and transfers behind one account-state map, then regression-test every operation after each extension.

Add spending totals and a deterministic top-N query while keeping the tie-break rule isolated so it can follow the supplied contract.

Model pending payments in time order and route all time-sensitive operations through one settle(now) transition.

Practice merging two account histories without losing the timeline needed for point-in-time balance queries.
