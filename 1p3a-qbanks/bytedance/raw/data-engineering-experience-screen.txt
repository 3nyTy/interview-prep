Requirements

Describe your data-modeling experience supporting analytics, reporting, and data applications.

Discuss your data-warehouse experience, including whether you have worked with Hive, Spark, Hadoop, or SQL Server.

Describe your experience with data services.

Notes

The format is Zoom or a phone call. The recruiter works through the background form item by item.

Organize each answer around the consumer, data grain, schema, ingestion path, quality checks, and one trade-off you personally handled. Separate tools used in production from tools known only conceptually.

Explain warehouse work through workload and storage choices, and data services through their consumers, freshness needs, interface, and failure handling. Use actual experience rather than invented scale or ownership.

Preparation

In 20 minutes, map each background-form topic to one real project and prepare a two-minute account of your responsibility and one concrete result.

In 15 minutes, sketch one analytics model and the path from ingestion to a consuming service; explain the grain, a quality check, and a design trade-off aloud.
