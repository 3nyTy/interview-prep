AI-Driven QA Pipeline Workflow
Design an AI-oriented QA workflow that turns a PRD into test cases, test data, automation scripts, execution, and a final report. The interviewer expects collaborating role-specific agents rather than merely inserting model calls into conventional CI stages, and asks how the system operates when CI has no GPU capacity.

Requirements

Accept a product-requirement document and derive accurate, high-adoption test cases.

Convert manual cases into maintainable automated scripts.

Generate or acquire the required test data for each case.

Execute functional and regression testing and produce a final test report.

Compose existing PRD-to-case, case-to-data, and data-to-script capabilities into one end-to-end workflow.

Model collaboration among PM, development, and QA responsibilities rather than treating each model call as an isolated CI stage.

Explain evaluation and control points that protect case accuracy, relevance, and adoption.

Operate when the CI/CD environment itself has no GPU resources.

Preserve extensibility and maintainability as product requirements and test skills evolve.

Examples

For a TikTok Shop feature, a creator uploads image-and-text content, associates an anchor and product link, and a consumer follows that link to view and purchase the product. Walk from the PRD through case design, data generation, automation, execution, and the final report.

Notes

A traditional Jenkins-style chain of independent stages is insufficient unless the design explains how specialized agents exchange plans, artifacts, feedback, and completion state.

Accuracy and adoption require grounding in requirements and business rules, coverage checks, executable validation, traceability from failures back to requirements, and human approval points for risky changes.

GPU absence is a deployment constraint, not permission to omit the AI layer; separate orchestration and CI execution from remotely hosted or shared inference capacity.

The design portion occupied about thirteen minutes before a long coding block, so lead with the workflow contract and decision points before expanding infrastructure.

Preparation

Draw the artifact and control flow from PRD to report, labeling ownership, validation gates, retries, and human approvals.

Define measurable quality signals for generated cases and scripts, then explain how failures feed back into the responsible component.

Rehearse two deployment shapes for GPU-constrained CI: remote inference behind a service boundary and an asynchronously scheduled shared model pool.
