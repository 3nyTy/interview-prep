Requirements

Walk through the details of an LLM safety post-training project and identify the parts you personally owned.

Explain which situations require escalation.

Defend the concrete design, the reasons behind it, and its trade-offs.

Compare current multimodal safety approaches and their trade-offs.

Notes

This was a one-hour background and technical interview. The team clarified its needs during the discussion, and those needs were not centered on LLM post-training.

Structure the project explanation around the threat model, your personal decisions, data and evaluation design, failure cases, and mitigation trade-offs. Distinguish measured results from hypotheses and team work from individual contributions.

For escalation, describe severity, uncertainty, available containment, and who can authorize the next action using your actual project policy. Do not invent universal thresholds.

Compare text-only and multimodal evaluation through modality-specific failures, cross-modal interactions, false refusals, and missed unsafe outputs. Explain where an input/output filter or post-training change helps and what it leaves unresolved.

Preparation

In 20 minutes, write a one-page project narrative with one design choice, one rejected alternative, and one real failure; label your ownership and supporting evidence.

In 15 minutes, rehearse an escalation case and build a small comparison table for two safety approaches, covering error types, latency, and evaluation gaps without inventing results.
