Requirements

Implement time-indexed key-value storage.

Notes

Twenty-five-minute second-round coding segment involving time-indexed storage after thirty-five minutes of technical questions.

For canonical practice, set(key, value, timestamp) writes at strictly increasing times. get(key, timestamp) returns the latest value no later than the query, or an empty string when absent. Append timestamp/value pairs per key and binary-search the first time greater than the query; its predecessor is the answer. Writes are amortized O(1), queries O(log(m + 1)) for m versions of that key, with O(N) total storage. Out-of-order writes require a different insertion strategy.

Preparation

Implement append and predecessor lookup in 20 minutes.

In 10 minutes, test absent keys and times before, on, between, and after writes.
