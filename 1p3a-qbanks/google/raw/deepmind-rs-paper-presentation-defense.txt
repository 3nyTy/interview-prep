Research Paper Presentation and Defense (DeepMind RS)
Present one of your papers, then defend the motivation, design choices, assumptions, alternatives, limitations, and next research steps. For multimodal/VLM work, expect close scrutiny of training and post-training depth and whether the contribution is more distinctive than evaluation or benchmark assembly.

Requirements

The round contains no coding. Present one of your own papers, then handle a fast sequence of research-defense questions:

Why was this problem worth pursuing, and why was the chosen framing appropriate?

Which alternative design could replace the one in the paper, and what would change?

What happens if a central assumption does not hold?

Why was each important design decision defensible?

What is the most credible next research direction?

Notes

Motivation and judgment carry more weight than reciting method details. Evaluation- or benchmark-heavy work is vulnerable when the training and post-training story is shallow. When a question cannot be answered conclusively, expose a defensible line of reasoning instead of bluffing.

Preparation

Reduce the paper to a concise narrative covering motivation, hypothesis, decisive design choices, evidence, and limitations.

Build an alternative-design table with credible rejected options, the trade-off behind each rejection, and the evidence that could reverse the decision.

Rehearse an adversarial defense in which every assumption is challenged and every answer ends with a falsifiable next experiment.
