Requirements

Given the head of a singly linked list, determine whether its values form a palindrome.

Use O(1) auxiliary space.

Follow-up: restore the linked list to its original structure before returning so callers do not observe a mutated input.

Notes

The space-bounded approach finds the midpoint with fast and slow pointers, reverses the second half, and compares the two halves. The engineering follow-up adds a second reversal after comparison to reconnect the original structure. Mentioning this side effect proactively demonstrates production-minded API discipline.

Preparation

Implement the odd-length and even-length cases without allocating an auxiliary value array.

Add restoration to the implementation, then write tests that compare the full node chain before and after the call.
