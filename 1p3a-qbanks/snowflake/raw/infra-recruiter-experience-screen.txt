Infra Recruiter Screen: Experience and Tooling

A sub-15-minute HR screen for an Infra role covering prior infrastructure experience, infrastructure or platform projects, and the tools and technology stack used. The recruiter then checks the candidate's fit with the target team before deciding the next step.

Infra Eng
infra
fit
Frequency
Single report
Last asked
2026-08-17
Stage
recruiter-screen
Requirements
Format: a recruiter / HR conversation lasting under 15 minutes.
Summarize your prior infrastructure experience.
Walk through infrastructure or platform projects you have built or supported.
Explain the tools and technology stack you used in that work.
Keep each answer concise enough for the recruiter to assess team fit within the short screen.
Notes
Structure each project summary around context, your exact ownership, scale or operating constraints, one reliability or delivery trade-off, and the outcome. This keeps the answer technical without turning a short screen into a full system-design walkthrough.
Tie each tool to a decision: what constraint made it appropriate, what alternative you considered, and what operational lesson you learned. A decision-oriented explanation is more informative than a list of product names.
Keep role alignment explicit by connecting the strongest project example to the infrastructure responsibilities you want to own next.
Preparation
Write and time a 60-second infrastructure background summary that names your scope, strongest platform area, and one concrete outcome; remove details that do not change the fit signal.
Prepare two separate two-minute project walkthroughs. For each, rehearse ownership, scale, failure mode, trade-off, and outcome without relying on slides or notes.
Make a one-page stack map for three tools you used: problem solved, why selected, rejected alternative, and one production lesson. Practice explaining each row in 20 seconds.
