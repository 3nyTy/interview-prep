AI-Conducted Screening Interview

An automated AI interviewer conducts a short screen for general SWE applicants. It checks background and logistics, then adaptively deep-dives into projects, design decisions, conflicts, and workplace AI usage while recording camera, screen, and transcript.

Requirements
Automated AI-conducted screen sent to general SWE applicants; must be completed within a week of the invitation and runs about 20 minutes.
Requires microphone, camera, and full-screen-share permissions. The interface shows a live transcript of both the AI's speech and yours on the left and your camera feed on the right.
Opens with logistics and background questions such as willingness to relocate, work authorization, and a self-introduction.
The core is an adaptive experience deep-dive — the AI latches onto keywords in your answers and drills deeper:
describe two projects and answer project-specific follow-ups;
explain how you designed a project;
describe how you handled a conflict;
mention building something in Java → "what was the most challenging part?" → answer "distributed …" → "how did you solve that in the distributed setting?";
mention a project spanning Slack / Teams / email → "those are very different platforms with many differences — how did you handle that?"
Asks how your current company integrates AI tools into its workflow.
Closes by inviting your questions; the AI deflects specifics (visa sponsorship, internal AI tooling, and details beyond the job description) to the hiring team or company website.
Notes
The follow-ups are genuinely dynamic rather than scripted — any answer can spawn a deeper probe, so keep each story structured and be ready to go two or three levels deep on anything you raise.
Finish each answer in one coherent turn. A pause can cause the AI to begin its next response before you have finished speaking.
Treat the "how does your company use AI tools" question as a recurring beat; answer with concrete tools and a short rationale, not a deflection.
Because it records camera, screen, and transcript, this is an evaluated round, not a chatbot warm-up.
Preparation
Pre-write two or three project stories, each with a clear "most challenging part" branch you can expand on demand, and rehearse drilling each one two levels deeper.
Prepare one project-design explanation and one conflict story with explicit decisions, alternatives, and outcomes.
Prepare a concrete answer for your current AI-tool usage: which tools, for what, and roughly what share of your work.
Do a camera + microphone + full-screen-share dry run beforehand so the permission setup doesn't eat into the 20 minutes.
