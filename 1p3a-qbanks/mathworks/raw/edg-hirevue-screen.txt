EDG HireVue Screen
MathWorks EDG candidates repeatedly see a short HireVue-style screen before or alongside the HackerRank OA. The recurring prompts cover EDG motivation, a recent technical project or internship, and U.S. work authorization / sponsorship.

Requirements

Format: asynchronous video answers, usually with one minute of preparation and up to two recording attempts per prompt.

Expected prompts:

Explain your understanding of the EDG program and why the position interests you.

Describe a recent internship, project, or programming / engineering skill set that makes you a strong fit for EDG.

State whether you are authorized to work in the United States without restriction and whether you need sponsorship now or in the future; student-visa candidates may also be asked for OPT timing.

Internship variants add availability questions, including what interests you most about the internship and preferred internship timing.

Notes

The prompt set is highly stable across new-grad and internship pipelines.

The EDG answer should show that you understand the rotation/support nature of the program rather than treating it as a generic SWE opening.

Candidates can usually replay the first take before choosing whether to record a second take; starting the second take discards the first.

Preparation

Prepare a 60-90 second EDG pitch: technical support for MATLAB / Simulink users, rotation across groups, and why that matches your learning goals.

Prepare one project story with concrete tools, debugging decisions, and your personal contribution.

Write a precise sponsorship answer before recording so the immigration prompt stays factual and concise.
