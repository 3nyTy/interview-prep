Working-with-PM / XFN pitch experiences (XFN round)

Notes

Quiet HM is widely cited as a negative signal: an HM not actively selling the role tends not to be excited about the candidate. With a silent interviewer, don't passively answer BQ; proactively pitch passion and role fit.

BQ is the final gate — only unlocked after technical rounds pass, so reaching it is itself a positive signal.

'Why OpenAI' — don't be generic ('AGI / cool / mission'); tie it to your research direction, industry history, specific take on the product line.

Preparation

STAR templates covering: conflict, failure, impact, leadership, cross-team

'Why OpenAI' — prepare 2-3 versions (research / safety / product / infra)

Read OpenAI's latest model releases, blog posts, safety papers

Practice a 'HM is silent' scenario; rehearse actively pitching
