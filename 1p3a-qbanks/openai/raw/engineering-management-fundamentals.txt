Examples

Introduction and background

Introduce yourself and summarize your background.

Is this your first management role?

How has your team size evolved during your management tenure?

Management philosophy and operations

What is your management philosophy?

How do you use one-on-one meetings?

How do you split time between coding and management, and how do you structure your calendar to maintain that balance?

Hiring and sourcing

Have you hired someone externally into your team?

What criteria and hiring bar did you apply?

How did you source that person: recruiter pipeline, referral, or another channel?

Mentorship and career growth

Give a concrete example of helping a junior engineer or new graduate grow.

What did the path and timeline to L4 look like?

What is the path from L4 to L5, and how do the expectations differ?

Performance management

Have you reorganized a team or managed someone out?

How do you run performance management within the company's culture?

Describe a low-performing team member: what was wrong, was it a skill mismatch, and what coaching or support did you provide?

Team dynamics and composition

How do you evaluate whether the team as a whole meets your performance bar?

Have you changed team structure even when individual performance was strong?

How did you identify the underlying skill gap?

Notes

The round spans both management philosophy and concrete operating history. Answers should distinguish personal decisions from team outcomes and make the timeline, intervention, and result explicit.

Preparation

Map one quantified story to each axis: hiring, mentorship, performance correction, reorganization, and team-level delivery.

Rehearse your management philosophy, one-on-one cadence, and coding-versus-management time allocation with specific examples rather than generic principles.
