Examples

Walk through your background, resume, and most recent project.

Why OpenAI, and which direction do you prefer: product-facing or infrastructure?

What is your compensation expectation, hiring timeline, and status with other interview processes?

How have you led people?

Describe a technically difficult project you helped solve.

Tell me about a project conflict and how you handled it.

What project are you most proud of?

Notes

HR runs standard BQ + process intro (timeline, salary expectations, location).

Typical timeline: recruiter reachout → HR call → phone screen usually 2-3 weeks apart.

HR won't share specific feedback — this is a stated company policy, not an oversight.

HR may ask for internal references very early. One candidate was asked on Day 1 post-interview, which is widely read as a borderline-status signal — neither pass nor reject confirmed.

Preparation

Standard BQ: why OpenAI / why leave / impact / failure

Don't reveal your reference network (OpenAI HR has been seen probing)

Salary expectations — research market; HR will frame one week to accept offer, but two is negotiable in practice
