Extensible Cinema Ticket Pricing
Design and implement an extensible cinema price calculator. Price combines a movie-format base price, optional add-on prices, a showtime multiplier, and a seat-tier multiplier.

Requirements

Movie format (standard, 3D, IMAX, and future formats) determines the base price.

Showtime (morning, afternoon, night, and future slots) supplies a multiplier.

Seat tier (standard, premium, VIP, and future tiers) supplies another multiplier.

A ticket may include zero or more fixed-price add-ons such as seat selection, extra legroom, or a blanket.

Compute price = (base price + sum(add-on prices)) × showtime multiplier × seat-tier multiplier.

New formats, showtimes, seat tiers, and add-ons should be introduced without rewriting existing pricing logic.

Notes

Keep configurable prices and multipliers outside conditional-heavy orchestration code.

Clarify currency precision, rounding, and whether promotions or taxes are inside the requested scope.

Model the base-price rule, showtime multiplier, seat-tier multiplier, and each add-on behind small pricing interfaces or configuration-backed strategies. The calculator composes their outputs and owns only rounding and final currency conversion.

Register new rule implementations through factories or dependency injection so extension adds a class/config entry rather than another branch in the calculator.

Preparation

Implement the object model and add one new movie format and add-on without modifying the calculator.

Write tests for no add-ons, multiple add-ons, each multiplier boundary, and currency rounding.
