SWE Resume Deep Dive: Technologies and Outcome Metrics
The opening portion of a 45-minute SWE phone screen drills into older resume projects: exactly what the candidate did, which technologies were used, and how the results were measured. Roughly 30 minutes of C++ debugging and coding followed this discussion.

Requirements

Select an older project or experience directly from the resume and explain it at implementation depth.

State precisely what work you performed, even when the experience is several years old.

Identify the concrete technologies used.

Explain how the result was measured and which evidence established the outcome.

Notes

This deep dive opened a 45-minute SWE phone screen before roughly 30 minutes of C++ debugging and coding.

Older resume bullets remain fair game; recency is not a reason to omit implementation details.

Preparation

Annotate every resume bullet, especially older ones, with your exact contribution and the technologies you used.

For each claimed result, write down the metric and how it was measured, then rehearse stating both without reconstructing them on the spot.

Practice a concise project explanation that can absorb detailed follow-ups without consuming the coding portion of the screen.
