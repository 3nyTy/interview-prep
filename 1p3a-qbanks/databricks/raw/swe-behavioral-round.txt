SWE / MLE Behavioral Round
Prepare for motivation, proud-project impact, project delays, difficult team pain points, feedback or technical disagreements, and cross-functional collaboration. Answers should make individual ownership, judgment, and measurable outcomes explicit.

Requirements

Prepare clear, specific answers for these prompts:

Describe your current role and explain your motivation for joining Databricks.

Tell the interviewer about a project you are most proud of, including your individual impact.

Explain a project delay, how you handled it, and what changed afterward.

Describe feedback you disagreed with, including how you reacted.

Describe a technical disagreement with a coworker and how it was resolved.

Explain a painful problem for the team and what you did about it.

Give an example of cross-functional collaboration and how you aligned the participants.

Describe an unexpected on-call event, how you handled the surprise, and how you coordinated the response.

Notes

The round combines motivation, ownership, receptiveness to feedback, technical conflict resolution, and cross-functional judgment. Keep the project context concrete enough that the interviewer can distinguish your individual contribution from the team's work.

Preparation

Draft a 90-second STAR outline for each listed prompt; label the context, your individual action, the outcome, and what you learned.

Rehearse the proud-project, delay, and technical-disagreement answers aloud, then remove any sentence that obscures your personal contribution or how the issue was resolved.

Write one sentence explaining how disputed feedback changed your later behavior, and practice delivering it without blaming the other person.
