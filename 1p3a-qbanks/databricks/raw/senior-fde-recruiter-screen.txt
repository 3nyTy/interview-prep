Senior FDE Recruiter Screen
The recruiter asks for the candidate's exact city, why they applied to the FDE role, why their background fits it, and how well they understand Databricks before opening the call for candidate questions.

Requirements

Prepare concise answers for the following recruiter prompts:

State your current location at city-level so the recruiter can validate it against the resume.

Explain why you applied to this specific role.

Explain why your background fits the role.

Describe what you know about Databricks.

Bring questions about the role and team; one version described the position as a re-profiled data-engineering organization seeking stronger full-stack capability.

Notes

This is a fit screen rather than a technical exercise. Role-specific motivation and concrete company knowledge are evaluated early in the call.

Preparation

Rehearse a three-minute screen covering location, role motivation, fit, and company knowledge without reading from notes.

Draft a 60-second fit answer that connects two relevant accomplishments to the work described in the role.

Write three questions about the role and team, including one that clarifies the boundary between data-engineering and full-stack responsibilities.
